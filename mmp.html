Project Overview

Malik Marketplace (MMP) - Second Hand Marketplace Platform

Physical resale shopping can be time-consuming, while many households have valuable unused items sitting idle. There was a need for a trusted platform that could connect buyers seeking affordable products with sellers looking to give their items a second life.

My team and I built Malik Marketplace (MMP), a web-based second-hand marketplace that streamlines buying, selling, and product management through dedicated customer, seller, and admin experiences.

Context

Imagine every unused item in your home had a second chance.

While buyers search for affordable alternatives to expensive products, many valuable items sit unused in homes, garages, and storage rooms. The challenge isn't supply or demand it's connecting the two.

That's where Malik Marketplace (MMP) comes in. Our team created a trusted online marketplace that helps people buy, sell, and give products a second life.

Problem

A fragmented resale experience.

Second-hand buyers often spend significant time searching across different stores and platforms to find affordable products, while sellers have limited ways to reach trustworthy buyers. Existing solutions lack structure, verification, and transparency, creating friction and reducing trust for both sides of the marketplace.

Goal

Make second-hand commerce simple, safe, and sustainable

Our goal was to create a secure and user-friendly marketplace that makes buying and selling second-hand products simple, trustworthy, and accessible. The platform needed to support customers, sellers, and administrators while promoting sustainable and affordable shopping.

Result

Delivered a Fully Functional Marketplace

The project successfully delivered a web-based marketplace that supports the complete second-hand buying and selling journey. Customers can browse products, manage favorites, add items to their cart, and make purchases, while sellers can create and manage listings through a dedicated dashboard.

The platform also includes administrative tools for category management, product approval, and user moderation, helping maintain trust and marketplace quality. By combining role-based access, product verification, and an intuitive user experience, the final solution created a reliable environment for second-hand commerce.
