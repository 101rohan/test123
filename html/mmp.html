Project Overview

Malik Marketplace (MMP) - Second Hand Marketplace Platform

Physical resale shopping can be time-consuming, while many households have valuable unused items sitting idle. There was a need for a trusted platform that could connect buyers seeking affordable products with sellers looking to give their items a second life.

My team and I built Malik Marketplace (MMP), a web-based second-hand marketplace that streamlines buying, selling, and product management through dedicated customer, seller, and admin experiences.

Context

Imagine every unused item in your home had a second chance.

While buyers search for affordable alternatives to expensive products, many valuable items sit unused in homes, garages, and storage rooms. The challenge isn't supply or demand it's connecting the two.

That's where Malik Marketplace (MMP) comes in. Our team created a trusted online marketplace that helps people buy, sell, and give products a second life.

Problem

Identifying the existing problem

It optimized for showing maximum products, not for making products easy to process.

• Cluttered layout - Overwhelming with too many promotional banners, offers, and categories.
• Poor product discoverability - Users struggle to find specific products or categories due to lack of organization and hierarchy.

Design Solution

1 - Product Density Changed

2 - Clear info Hierarchy

Less information per screen → easier scanning → stronger focus.

3 - Reduced Choice Overload

Separate categories improve conversion because they reduce friction between wanting something and finding something.

Reflection

Simplicity wins

The redesign focused on improving information hierarchy and product discoverability. The original interface contained heavily packed feeds, reducing users' ability to process content. These changes improve scanning efficiency and support faster product discovery.
